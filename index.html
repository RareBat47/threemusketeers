Flagship Product — MVP in Development

A personal trading concierge, in your home.

Active traders are surrounded by information but starved of personal context. Our voice-first assistant interviews the trader — what they trade, their style, risk appetite, goals — then learns their behaviour and portfolio over time to deliver daily briefings, trade reviews, and behavioural insight, entirely by voice, on major smart home ecosystems.

Voice-first

Designed for major smart home ecosystems, so the assistant lives where traders already are — at home, hands-free.

Learns the trader

Builds a structured trader profile — behaviour, patterns, portfolio — so every answer stays consistent with their style.

Security by design

Sensitive financial data is encrypted at rest and in transit, with strict per-user isolation. Assistance first — never order execution by voice.

Offered as a subscription through smart home platforms. A functional prototype is under active development, with private beta planned next.
